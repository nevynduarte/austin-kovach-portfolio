About Austin Kovach

Curiosity, made tangible.

Austin Kovach is an industrial and product designer in Bedminster, New Jersey, and a recent Montclair State University graduate with a BFA in Industrial Design. He is fascinated by how design can communicate ideas, evoke emotion, and create meaningful experiences through user interaction.

His work spans product design, concept development, 3D modeling, rendering, prototyping, and visual communication, with a creative and user-focused approach to problem solving.
